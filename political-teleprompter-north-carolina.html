Political Teleprompter Questions

Can you handle a script that changes an hour before?

Yes. We load, format and edit live from the desk, including cuts and inserts while the speaker is already on stage.

Do you work election night?

Yes. Both speeches loaded, a holding script for the wait, on site until the speaker is done.

Is the prompter visible to the audience or cameras?

No. The speaker reads two clear glass panels beside the podium; the room and cameras see a speaker looking at them.

How much does it cost?

Single-speech events are quoted per event, operator and equipment included. Multi-day and multi-city schedules are quoted as a package. You get the number the same day.
